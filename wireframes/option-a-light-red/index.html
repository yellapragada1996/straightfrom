UI mockup, round 2. Same UX structure (items first, creator up top, brand in the background), now with character: fan-poster type, the creator's own words in italic serif, a red story ring, a live ticker and a bold red Buy button. The frames are live pages, so you can scroll, swipe photos and click through. The phone frames show how fans see the pages inside Instagram's in-app browser.

Creator page

  • Face, name and @handle first: fans confirm they're in the right place in one second.
  • Items fill the page: 2 columns on phones, 4 on desktop. Photo, title and price only.
  • Quantity only appears when there's more than one ("2 available").
  • Sold items stay, grouped at the end. They show that things actually sell.
  • Social links as proof it's really them. No fake verified badge.
  • Brand kept small: a wordmark and a "Sell it on StraightFrom" footer.
